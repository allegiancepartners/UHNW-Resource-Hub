A "zeroed-out" GRAT: the annuity is sized so its actuarial value equals the funding amount, leaving little to no taxable gift. Anything the trust earns above the §7520 rate passes to beneficiaries free of gift and estate tax. Works best with a short term on an asset expected to outperform the hurdle rate. Mortality risk: if Eleanor Whitfield doesn't survive the term, the trust reverts to the taxable estate.

A modest seed gift (using exemption) funds a grantor trust, which then buys an asset for an interest-only note at the AFR. No gain is recognized (grantor trust), and the sale itself uses almost no exemption. Growth above the note rate compounds for beneficiaries, transfer-tax-free.

Valuation Discounting

A minority (lack-of-control) and marketability discount, typically applied through a family limited partnership or LLC, reduces the value used for gift-tax reporting purposes below the pro-rata fair market value of the underlying asset — meaning more can be transferred per dollar of exemption used. Eleanor Whitfield's working assumption is a 30% combined discount; an independent appraisal is required to support it on audit.

Buy-Sell Agreement & Succession

Status on file: In place — unfunded. A funded buy-sell agreement fixes a value and mechanism for a future ownership transfer — voluntary, involuntary, or at death — and is foundational to any succession plan, family or third-party.

Existing Trust Inventory & Family Governance

Eleanor Whitfield is a beneficiary of one or more existing trusts on file. Understanding each trust's distribution standard, any powers of appointment held, and how decisions are coordinated with co-beneficiaries shapes what additional planning makes sense — this is reviewed in full in Appendix J of the Reference Library.

After the trust is funded, its assets can keep compounding under an Estate Freeze — a well-established structure built on a Grantor or Non-Grantor Trust election. If Eleanor Whitfield elects grantor trust status, they pay the trust's income tax personally each year — the trust grows tax-free, and the payment itself is treated as an additional tax-free gift to the trust. Combined with the assets sitting entirely outside the taxable estate, this can meaningfully outperform holding everything personally.
