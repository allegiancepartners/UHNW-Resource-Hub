Estate Planning & Professional Credentials

Where to find credentialed estate planning professionals, and the credentials themselves worth recognizing on an advisor's resume.

NAEPC — National Association of Estate Planners & Councils

The national network of affiliated local estate planning councils and credentialed professionals. Awards the Accredited Estate Planner® (AEP®) designation and maintains a searchable directory of credentialed advisors.

Visit naepc.org →

STEP — Society of Trust and Estate Practitioners

A global professional association for practitioners who specialize in family inheritance and succession planning, with local branches and special interest groups including philanthropy advisory.

Visit step.org →
Family Office Community

Peer networks and learning platforms built specifically for multi-generational families and the professionals who advise them.

Family Office Exchange (FOX)

The industry-leading membership organization connecting families, family office executives, and trusted advisors — peer networking, education, research, and an advisor directory, serving multi-generational wealthy families since 1989.

Visit familyoffice.com →

Purposeful Planning Institute

A nonprofit learning and networking platform for advisors working with families of wealth and family enterprises — estate attorneys, CPAs, financial advisors, trust officers, and family business consultants.

Visit purposefulplanninginstitute.com →
Philanthropy & Charitable Giving

Resources for families structuring charitable giving as part of their broader wealth transfer plan.

Council on Foundations

A nonprofit membership association supporting private, corporate, and community foundations — advocacy, legal guidance, and educational resources for grantmakers of every size.

Visit cof.org →

American Council on Gift Annuities (ACGA)

Established in 1927, ACGA publishes the actuarially sound suggested maximum charitable gift annuity rates relied on by the vast majority of US charitable organizations.

Visit acga.org →
