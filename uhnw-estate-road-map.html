Wealth Drivers
The 8 Drivers of Wealth Continuity
What actually determines how efficiently a family's wealth reaches the next generation, independent of the raw net worth number.
These 8 Drivers of Wealth Continuity are the factors that determine how efficiently a family's wealth actually reaches the next generation — independent of how large the balance sheet is. Score each driver 1 (weak) to 10 (strong) to see how it moves the overall Wealth Continuity Score and, on the Gifting Estimator tab, the pace of wealth transfer the family's current readiness can support.
